Headline

Dr Stephen J Newhouse, PhD. Head of Professional Services & Senior Bioinformatician at Biorelate Ltd.

20+ years across academia, NHS, and industry: bioinformatics, ML, target, drug, and biomarker discovery, knowledge graphs, agentic AI.

📍 Manchester, England, UK. Open to hybrid / remote.

LinkedIn · GitHub · ORCID · Google Scholar · KCL Pure

Top skills

Top 3 (LinkedIn): Agile Methodologies · Data Management · Natural Language Processing (NLP).

  • Languages: Python, R, Bash, SQL.
  • ML / data: scikit-learn, PyCaret, Prophet, MLflow, pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, classical stats, time-series, biostats.
  • NLP & knowledge graphs: biomedical text mining, entity and relation extraction, graph-based reasoning.
  • LLM / agentic AI: RAG, agent memory, tool-use, evaluation, MCP, Anthropic / OpenAI / HuggingFace stacks.
  • Bioinformatics: NGS (DNA/RNA), variant calling, GWAS, RNA-seq, multi-omics, drug repurposing, Illumina arrays.
  • Pipelines & infra: Snakemake, Nextflow, Docker, Conda; Git/GitHub; CI/CD.
  • Cloud: AWS, GCP.
  • Leadership: team building, client engagement, training, consulting, cross-functional delivery, Agile.

Languages

  • English. Native or bilingual.

Experience

Head of Professional Services & Senior Bioinformatician · Biorelate Ltd.

June 2021 – present · United Kingdom (Manchester)

  • Run client onboarding, training, and product trials. Make sure people actually use what we ship and get real value out of it.

Now integrated into large-pharma clients’ own internal LLM and tech stacks, so they get agentic biomedical reasoning natively where their data already lives.
  • Led delivery of a target safety assessment app for a life-sciences pharma client. Knowledge-graph and LLM-driven evidence retrieval, programmatic citation, regulator-ready outputs.
  • Designed and ran a Langdock integration evaluation for Biorelate: scoping, validation, and integration patterns for using Langdock as a managed-AI platform alongside our own stack.
